Delete your account

In the app (fastest)

Open DIY Car Club → My Garage → Settings → Account & sign-in → Delete account. You may be asked to sign in again. Deletion happens immediately.

Without the app

Email jaysuh2020@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us which sign-in method you used). We confirm ownership before deleting and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, cars, work logs, posts, comments, photos, saved items, settings and device tokens. Messages you sent are erased; the other person keeps the conversation thread without your content.
