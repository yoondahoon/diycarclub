DIY Car Club

Fix your car together.

Friendly help for every driver — beginners welcome. DIY Car Club is a community app for people who want to look after their own cars.

  • See common problems for your car, with plain-language fixes and how-to videos.
  • Ask anything — no question is too basic.
  • Meet people who drive the same car and help each other.

The iPhone app is in testing. Android is coming next.
