DIY Car Club

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what DIY Car Club ("we", "the app") collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile: the username, display name, photo and bio you choose.
  • Your cars: make, model, year and anything you add, such as mileage or work logs.
  • Content you post: posts, comments, photos, repair requests and reviews.
  • Messages: direct messages you send and receive, so the conversation can be delivered.
  • Approximate location: if you allow it, we use your approximate location once to suggest the nearest city. We store the city you choose, not your exact position.
  • Device: a push-notification token if you turn on notifications, and basic technical data (app version, language) needed to run the service.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, showing your profile and posts, delivering messages and notifications.
  • To show content relevant to you, such as common problems for your car and members with the same car (only cars you mark as public are shown to others).
  • To keep the community safe: handling reports, blocking, and preventing spam and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage (United States).
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them.
  • Expo / Apple / Google deliver push notifications.
  • YouTube: how-to videos are played with YouTube's own player. When you play a video, YouTube may collect data under the Google Privacy Policy.

What other members can see

Your username, display name, photo, public cars, posts and comments are visible to other members. Your email address, private cars and messages are not. You can block members and limit who can message you in Settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (My Garage → Settings → Account & sign-in → Delete account) or from the account deletion page. Deletion removes your profile, cars, posts, photos and settings; limited records may be kept where the law requires (for example, abuse reports) and are then removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU/UK or South Korea), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request; we will respond within the time the law requires.

Children

DIY Car Club is not intended for children under 13 (under 14 in South Korea), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
